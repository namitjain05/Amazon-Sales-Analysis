# Amazon Product Sales Analysis Dashboard 📊

This Power BI project provides a detailed sales performance analysis of Amazon products. It focuses on key metrics such as sales trends, product reviews, and top-performing categories to support strategic decision-making in sales and marketing.

## 🔍 Objective

To monitor and evaluate Amazon product performance over time by analyzing year-to-date and quarterly sales, product movement, and customer feedback. The dashboard is built to uncover actionable insights that help identify top revenue drivers and areas for improvement.

## 📌 KPIs Tracked

- **YTD Sales**: Monitor year-to-date sales to evaluate overall revenue performance.
- **QTD Sales**: Track quarterly sales to identify fluctuations and trends.
- **YTD Products Sold**: Analyze the number of products sold during the year.
- **YTD Reviews**: Track customer feedback to measure satisfaction and engagement.

## 📊 Visuals Included

- **YTD Sales by Month (Line Chart)**: Monthly sales trend analysis to observe seasonal patterns.
- **YTD Sales by Week (Column Chart)**: Weekly performance overview for short-term insights.
- **Sales by Product Category (Text/Heat Map)**: Sales distribution across different categories.
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Sales (Bar Chart)**: Identify highest-selling products.
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews (Bar Chart)**: Understand customer preferences via reviews.

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies Used

- **Power BI Desktop (.pbix)**
- **DAX** for calculated measures
- **Amazon product sales dataset** (structure not shared)

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Download** the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. **Open** it in Power BI Desktop.
3. **Refresh** the data if connected to a source or review the visuals.
4. Explore interactive dashboards with slicers and filters to derive insights.
